Privacy Policy for Tic Tac Pro

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Tic Tac Pro ("the App") is developed by LuciferBalot. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and shared when you use the App.

Information We Collect

Tic Tac Pro does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number or email address to play the game.

The App may use third-party services, including Google AdMob, to display advertisements. These services may collect information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, IP address and information about interactions with advertisements, as permitted by applicable laws and their own privacy policies.

How We Use Information

Information collected by third-party advertising services may be used to provide, maintain and improve advertising services, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and provide advertisements.

Third-Party Services

The App may use Google AdMob for advertising. Google's privacy policy is available here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

Tic Tac Pro is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact the developer so appropriate action can be taken.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Tic Tac Pro does not maintain a user account or a developer-owned database containing personal information submitted by users. Information collected by third-party services is handled according to those services' policies and retention practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the App, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Tic Tac Pro Google Play listing.
